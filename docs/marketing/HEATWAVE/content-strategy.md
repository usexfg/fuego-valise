# Heatwave Content Strategy

*Status: draft — founder-directed*

## Content pillars

1. **Private exchange** — self-custody, atomic execution, clear swap state.
2. **Sound money** — purchasing-power narrative without yield promises.
3. **House identity** — rarity, restraint, cultural institution.
4. **Operational honesty** — SPV limits, RPC requirements, unreleased status.

## Editorial rules

- Every piece carries the fictional-teaser disclaimer.
- Sell identity and restraint, not APY, TPS, fees, or features.
- Technical claims must match AGENTS.md and code-backed docs.
- If an image cannot pass black-and-white review, replace it.
