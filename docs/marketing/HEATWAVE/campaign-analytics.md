# Heatwave Campaign Analytics — Review Method

## Bottom line

Use analytics to answer three questions: did the teaser travel, did it convert attention to hub reads, and did it confuse anyone.

## Funnel

Awareness → teaser view → disclaimer view → email/social click → official-channel follow.

Report stage-to-stage conversion and largest drop. Compare email vs social segments.

## Attribution

Compare at least first-touch, last-touch, and linear models across:

- Owned email/community
- Rented social
- Borrowed partner/community links

No single model decides budget; use triangulation.

## ROI

ROI is attention-based for a teaser, not revenue-based. Track:

- Cost per teaser-hub view, if paid testing is approved.
- Cost per qualified hub read/disclaimer view.
- Cost per official-channel follow.
- Misunderstanding rate by creative variant.

Include creative, tooling, and labor costs if paid spend occurs. Do not fabricate benchmarks.

## Weekly review

- Spend vs cap.
- Top/bottom creative by hub clicks and misunderstanding rate.
- Frequency/fatigue signals.
- Landing-message match: ad promise versus hub disclaimer.
- Decision: pause, iterate, or keep paused.
