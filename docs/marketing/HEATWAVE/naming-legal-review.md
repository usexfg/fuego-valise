# Heatwave Naming Review

*Date: 2026-09-14*
*Status: decided — not legal advice*

## Decision

**“Bank of XFG” is retired.** The house name is **Obsidian Reserve**.

**“L'Avoir XFG” is not used.** As a brand it reads as “the XFG holdings,” which is poetic but awkward next to Maison-style naming. Obsidian Reserve is cleaner and already matches the palette.

## Naming architecture

1. **Product truth:** Fuego Valise
2. **House name:** Obsidian Reserve
3. **Release teaser:** Heatwave
4. **English descriptor:** sound-money reserve (descriptor only, not a second brand)
5. **Avoid as operative names:** Bank of XFG, Sound Money Bank, XFG Bank, XFG Trust, XFG Bancorp

## Why “Bank” was retired

Several U.S. state statutes restrict “bank” / “banking” / “banker” use by unauthorized persons, and several cover individuals, firms, associations, and legal persons — not only corporations. Examples consulted: Washington RCW 30A.04.020; Nebraska Rev. Stat. § 8-113; Illinois 205 ILCS 5/46. Separately, FDIC rules at 12 CFR Part 328 Subpart B target false or misleading deposit-insurance representations by any person.

Retiring the word removes the issue instead of disclaimering around it.

## Remaining rules

- No deposits, accounts, insurance, yield, charter, or release claims in campaign assets.
- Teaser status (“preview / unreleased”) stays on the hub and hero visual only — not repeated on every page.
- Revisit with counsel before any app-store, domain, trademark, or paid-search use of a financial-sounding name.
