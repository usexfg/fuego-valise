*Tier: teaser / Tier 3 preview*
*Campaign: Obsidian Reserve — v1.11 Heatwave*
*Status: draft — founder-directed*

## Bottom line

Run Heatwave as a **short, controlled teaser**: owned channels first, rented social second, borrowed distribution only after disclaimers are approved. Do not buy broad paid reach until legal, app-store, and tracking risks are cleared.

## Phase model

### Prelaunch

1. Lock naming, disclaimer, and lexicon.
2. Finalize teaser copy and visual direction.
3. Prepare social/email assets and UTM taxonomy.
4. Run brand audit and fix blocking failures.
5. Decide whether paid amplification is allowed.

### Launch day

- Publish campaign hub.
- Send email 1.
- Post X thread and LinkedIn article.
- Monitor replies for banking/insurance/yield misunderstandings.
- Correct misleading reposts quickly.

### Postlaunch — 30 days

- Publish comparison/explainer: self-custody vs custodial exchange.
- Publish behind-the-scenes brand post.
- Send roundup email.
- Retarget only if tracking and consent are properly implemented.
- Archive results and decide whether Heatwave graduates to a real release campaign.

## Launch-day checklist

- [ ] Teaser status (“preview / unreleased”) clear on hub and hero visual
- [ ] No “released,” “live,” “APY,” “insured,” or “deposit” claims
- [ ] UTM parameters use agreed taxonomy
- [ ] Social replies monitored for compliance misunderstandings
- [ ] Paid ads remain paused unless explicitly approved
- [ ] Tracker updated

## Readiness gate

Do not expand beyond owned + limited rented channels unless all are true:

- [ ] House name is Obsidian Reserve everywhere.
- [ ] No app-store/domain/trademark use of “Bank of XFG.”
- [ ] Measurement plan exists.
- [ ] Brand audit passes or has accepted waivers.

## Success metrics

- Teaser hub views and email open/click rates.
- Social saves/shares and thoughtful replies.
- Misunderstanding rate: replies assuming banking, insurance, yield, or release.
- Zero paid/compliance incidents.
